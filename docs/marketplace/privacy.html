Privacy Policy

ResearchSignal Table is an Excel add-in for statistical analysis of research tables. This policy describes the current pilot build.

Draft for publisher review. Before Marketplace submission, replace the publisher contact placeholder and verify this policy against the final hosting, analytics, support, and cloud-service configuration.

Workbook content and calculations

The add-in reads the range, labels, and headers that you select or ask it to inspect in Excel. Statistical calculations run in the add-in. Results and formatting are written to the workbook only when you choose an operation that changes it. The current build does not upload workbook contents to a ResearchSignal server.

Settings

You can choose not to save settings or to save them locally in the Excel add-in's browser storage on that device. Local settings are not synchronized between devices. The current build does not provide cloud settings synchronization, email sign-in, or an account service. If these capabilities are added in a later version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Information processed by Microsoft

The add-in runs inside Microsoft Excel and is subject to Microsoft's privacy terms and the policies of the Microsoft 365 organization you use. Microsoft may process information needed to provide Office, the Office Add-ins platform, and hosting of the add-in files. Review Microsoft's Privacy Statement.

Support requests

If you submit a support ticket, the information you enter, including your reply email address and issue description, is sent to Web3Forms for delivery to the ResearchSignal support inbox. Web3Forms states that it processes form submissions using infrastructure providers that may operate in multiple regions, including the United States, and retains submission data for up to three years unless a shorter period applies or the data is deleted earlier. It also states that submitter IP and email addresses may be sent to its spam-prevention providers. Web3Forms processes the submission under its own privacy policy and data processing terms. Do not include workbook files, passwords, confidential research data, or other sensitive information. Review Web3Forms' Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

Data sharing, sale, and advertising

The current build has no ResearchSignal account service, analytics SDK, or advertising functionality. We do not sell personal information. The add-in does not transmit workbook contents to us. Support ticket information is shared with Web3Forms only to deliver your request.

Retention and deletion

Locally saved settings remain in the add-in's local browser storage until you clear or reset them, remove the add-in, or clear the relevant Office/browser data. We do not receive or retain those local settings.

Children

ResearchSignal Table is a productivity tool and is not designed for children.
