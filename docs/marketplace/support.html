ResearchSignal Table Support

ResearchSignal Table helps analyze statistical significance in research tables in Excel.

Support contact required before Marketplace publication. Replace this notice with a monitored support contact or support form URL. Microsoft requires a working HTTPS support page for Marketplace listings.

Installation

Open the Excel installation guide: Install ResearchSignal Table. If your organization hides the “Upload My Add-in” command, ask your Microsoft 365 administrator to deploy the add-in from its XML manifest.

Getting started

Select one continuous numeric data area in Excel, open the ResearchSignal tab, choose Open Table, and use the relevant calculation or check action. Read the project README for workflow details.

Report a problem

When contacting support, include your Excel version and platform, the add-in version, the action you attempted, and the exact error message. Do not send a workbook containing confidential or personal information. If a sample is necessary, replace its contents with synthetic data first.

Your request and reply email are delivered through Web3Forms to the ResearchSignal support inbox. The support email address is not included in this page. Do not include passwords, workbook files, personal research data, or other sensitive information. See our Privacy Policy.
